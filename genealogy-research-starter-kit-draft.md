# Family History Research Starter Kit — Draft Product Content

## Product positioning
A practical printable and fillable genealogy worksheet bundle for turning scattered records into organized ancestor profiles and family stories.

Genealogy Research Planner Printable PDF — Ancestor Profile, Cemetery Log, Family History Worksheets

Organize your family history research with printable genealogy worksheets for ancestor profiles, timelines, cemetery visits, source citations, research logs, and family interviews. Built for hobbyists who want to turn records into clear family stories.
