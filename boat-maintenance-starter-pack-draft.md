# Boat Owner Maintenance Starter Pack — Draft Product Content

## Product positioning
A simple printable/digital maintenance system for boat owners who want one place to track service, winterization, expenses, fuel, parts, and pre-launch checks.

## Proposed price
- Starter PDF: $9
- Expanded PDF + spreadsheet: $19
- Boat/RV/Van mega-bundle later: $29-$49

## Included pages/templates
1. Boat profile sheet
2. Engine/service profile
3. Annual maintenance calendar
4. Spring pre-launch checklist
5. Trip readiness checklist
6. Fuel log
7. Repair and service log
8. Parts and supplies tracker
9. Marina/slip/storage expense tracker
10. Winterization checklist
11. Battery maintenance log
12. Trailer maintenance log
13. Safety equipment checklist
14. Notes and lessons learned

## Draft listing title
Boat Maintenance Logbook Printable PDF — Winterization Checklist, Fuel Log, Repair Tracker, Marina Expense Planner

## Draft short description
Keep your boat maintenance, fuel, repairs, winterization, and marina expenses organized in one printable PDF system. Designed for everyday boat owners who want a simple, practical record before launch season, during lake weekends, and at winter storage time.
