# Niche Calculator Websites — MVP Specs

## Calculator 1: Boat Ownership Cost Calculator
Inputs: purchase price, loan/payment, slip/storage, insurance, fuel/month, maintenance/month, winterization, trailer costs, registration, accessories.
Outputs: monthly cost, annual cost, 5-year cost, printable summary, CTA to Boat Owner Maintenance Pack.

## Calculator 2: RV Trip Cost Calculator
Inputs: miles, MPG, fuel price, campground nights, food/day, maintenance reserve, activities, supplies.
Outputs: trip estimate, per-day cost, per-mile cost, packing checklist CTA.

## Calculator 3: Van Conversion Budget Calculator
Inputs: electrical, insulation, bed, cabinets, water, solar, tires, recovery gear, labor, contingency.
Outputs: low/medium/high estimate, category pie chart, CTA to Van Build Budget Tracker.

## Monetization
- Free tool page gets traffic.
- CTA sells $9-$29 templates/logs.
- Affiliate links can be added only after account/disclosure approval.
