# Nombo Income Faucet — Top-Tier 10 Business Plan Overview

**Mission:** Build ten small, mostly Nombo-operated income faucets in parallel.

**Revenue deadlines:**
- **By July 4, 2026:** at least one idea has non-zero revenue.
- **By July 31, 2026:** all ten ideas have non-zero revenue or a live, buyable offer with an explicit reason if revenue is still pending.

**Operating principle:** David contributes whenever he has time or brainstorms. Nombo keeps every lane warm by preparing assets, offers, copy, product backlogs, launch pages, and approval-ready decisions.

**Approval gates:** Nombo does not spend money, publish under David’s identity, contact real people, buy domains, run ads, accept client obligations, or move money without approval.

## Fastest path to non-zero revenue

The fastest likely first-dollar lane is **T1 Boat/RV maintenance digital download pack**, followed by **T2 Genealogy templates**, **T6 Prompt packs**, and **T5 Printable/template store**. These avoid Printful, KDP, marketplace approval, or customer meetings.

## Revenue strategy

Use one simple payment/download platform first, then reuse it across T1, T2, T5, T6, and T9. POD/KDP lanes can follow once account, banking, tax, T&Cs, and publishing approvals are complete.

## The 10 lanes

### T1. Boat/RV maintenance digital download pack
- **MVP:** One $9-$19 downloadable bundle: boat maintenance log, winterization checklist, pre-launch checklist, fuel/repair tracker, marina/slip expense tracker.
- **Primary channel:** Gumroad/Payhip/Etsy-style digital delivery + simple landing page
- **July 4 move:** Publish the Boat Owner Maintenance Starter Pack and ask 5-10 warm/nearby boating people to review/share/buy.
- **July 31 target:** Add RV and van versions; bundle all three; publish 3 SEO articles.
- **Nombo work:** Draft PDFs, checklists, product copy, landing page, email/social copy, SEO articles.
- **David work:** Sanity-check maintenance items, choose payment platform, approve publishing/pricing.
- **Pricing:** $9-$19 digital; $29 bundle
- **Non-zero revenue path:** Fastest path: sell one $9 pack.
