# First Calculator Website Spec

## Recommended first calculator
Boat Ownership Cost Calculator

## Why
It supports the boat maintenance pack, boating merch, affiliate content, and marina lead-gen.

## Inputs
- Boat type
- Purchase price or current value
- Slip/storage cost
- Insurance
- Fuel estimate
- Maintenance estimate
- Winterization/storage
- Trailer costs
- Accessories/safety gear
- Number of boating months

## Outputs
- Estimated annual cost
- Estimated monthly cost
- Estimated cost per boating day
- Maintenance budget recommendation
- CTA: download the Boat Owner Maintenance Starter Pack
